# Institutional Digital Asset Assurance v0.1.1

This patch release makes evidence freshness visible at the point of assessment. Evaluated evidence is labeled `STALE` when its observation date exceeds the control threshold, and the finding states the evidence age and configured limit. For the custody demonstration, the report explains that the evidence is 47 days old against a 30-day limit and that the engine cannot infer control effectiveness from it.

All demonstration evidence remains synthetic. See the [v0.1.0 release](https://github.com/vishnugovind10/institutional-digital-asset-assurance/releases/tag/v0.1.0) for the initial feature set and broader limitations.
